# Diffusion in an Active Medium — Lab Preparation & Literature Review

An original English preparation guide and literature review for the **"Brownian motion in an active medium"** experiment of the Physics Advanced Laboratory (1140250/1180076) at the Technion, Winter 2026–27.

**Read it here: https://yuvalhir.github.io/active-matter-lab/**

## Contents
- Equilibrium Brownian-motion theory (Langevin, MSD, Einstein relation, Stokes drag)
- Active media and the bacterial bath (run-and-tumble, swarming, effective temperature)
- A review of Wu & Libchaber, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **84**, 3017 (2000) — the experiment this lab follows
- The full lab protocol: samples, microscopy, particle tracking, MSD analysis, concentration dependence
- The optical-tweezers module (trap calibration, Stokes-drag viscometry)
- The briefing's preparation questions, with pointers
- Compact stat-mech-level refreshers (FDT, equipartition, measurement noise, microscopy essentials)
- Software links and practical notes
